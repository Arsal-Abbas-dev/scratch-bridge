# Testing Plan
 
## Who
5 to 8 beginners. Best: people who have used Scratch or other blocks, but not Python.
Anyone under 18 needs a parent or teacher to say OK first.
I write first names or initials only.
 
## What they do
They play levels 1 to 4 on the live site. It takes about 30 minutes.
 
## Rules for me
- I do not help for the first 2 minutes of someone being stuck.
- I do not explain how Python works until after the test.
- I write notes while they play.
 
## Questions before
1. Have you used Scratch or another block tool? (yes / no)
2. Have you written Python? (never / a little / a lot)
3. How sure are you that you could write code? (1 = not at all, 5 = very sure)
 
## Questions after
1. How sure are you now that you could write code? (1 to 5)
2. Quiz: What does "for _ in range(3):" do?
3. Quiz: What does turn_left() do?
4. Quiz: Why does a loop line end with a colon?
5. What was the hardest part?
6. What did you like?
